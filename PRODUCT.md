## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Claims:** Ground every project with verifiable repository links, architecture breakdowns, live URLs, or benchmark metrics.
2. **Authoritative Restraint:** Favor solid dark surfaces, crisp typographic tracking, and high-contrast accents over generic AI clichés.
3. **Dual-Domain Distinction:** Clearly delineate large-scale Data Infrastructure from Generative AI while demonstrating their convergence.
4. **Instant Comprehension:** Deliver the engineer's core value proposition within the first viewport on both mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliant text contrast against pure `#000000` and dark obsidian surfaces.
- Semantic HTML5 landmark structure (`header`, `main`, `section`, `footer`).
- Full keyboard navigability and visible focus rings.
- Proper image alt descriptions for portrait and iconography.
